Frequently Asked Questions

Do tickets include workshop access?

Yes. The Standard pass includes 2 workshop sessions, while Pro gives you unlimited access to every workshop across all 3 days.

Will the talks be recorded?

All 48 talks are professionally recorded. Standard ticket holders can rewatch them for 30 days, and Pro attendees get lifetime access.

Can I upgrade my ticket later?

Absolutely. You can move from Standard to Pro any time before the conference by paying only the price difference.

What is the refund policy?

Full refunds are available until August 15, 2026. After that, you can transfer your ticket to a friend or teammate free of charge.

Is there a student discount?

Yes. Students get 40% off the Standard pass — just bring a valid student ID when you pick up your badge.

Are meals included?

Every ticket covers the networking lunch and coffee breaks on all 3 days, with vegetarian, vegan, and halal options available.

How does the Team pass work?

One payment of $5,999 covers up to 10 teammates. Everyone gets full Pro benefits, plus your dedicated team lounge.

Can I speak at DevConf 2026?

We'd love that! Talk proposals are open until the end of September — submit yours through the Contact Us button above.
